# Operations
## Daily rhythm
Requesters ask for trips as early as they can. The controller clears the waiting list at the start of each shift and again after lunch. Drivers mark themselves available when on duty and share their position when they start a trip.

## Rules everyone follows
1. Always give a reason when rejecting a trip.
2. Cancel a trip as soon as it is not needed, so the driver is freed.
3. Drivers complete a trip as soon as they arrive.
