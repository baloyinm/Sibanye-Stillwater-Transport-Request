# Features
## For everyone
Status chips, a Next step line on every trip, a history of who did what, light and dark themes, phone-first layout, and the app opens offline for screens already loaded.

## Requester
Request a trip, optional cost centre, parts requests when switched on, in-app messages when switched on.

## Controller
Approve or reject, nearest-free-driver list, reports by status and vehicle, CSV download, places management.

## Driver
Availability, share position, accept or decline offers (when switched on), start and complete.
