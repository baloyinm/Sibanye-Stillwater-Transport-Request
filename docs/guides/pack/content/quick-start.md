# Quick start
## Requester
1. Sign in and tap Request a trip.
2. Choose where from and to, the vehicle and passengers, then Send request.
3. Open the trip any time to see its status.

## Controller
1. Open All trips. Open a waiting trip.
2. Approve trip or Reject trip (give a reason).
3. Choose a driver and Assign a driver.

## Driver
1. Tap Share my position when you start work.
2. Open your trip, Start the trip, then Mark trip complete.
